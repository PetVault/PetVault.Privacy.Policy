PetVault Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026  |  Package Name: com.petvault.app

🔒 Local-First Guarantee: PetVault is designed with privacy at its core. Your pet health records, medical certificates, vaccination logs, weight records, and photos are stored locally on your device. We do not operate cloud servers to store or aggregate your personal pet health records.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PetVault ("we," "our," or "us"). PetVault is a local-first mobile application designed to assist pet owners in managing pet profile information, vaccination histories, medical documents, weight growth charts, and treatment reminders.

By installing and using PetVault, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Local-First Data Storage Architecture

PetVault functions using an on-device, local-first data model:

  • On-Device SQLite Database: All pet profiles, vaccination records, weight entry history, and custom logs are stored locally on your device inside an isolated SQLite database and local file storage.
  • No Central Cloud Servers: We do not operate external application servers that harvest or store your personal pet health information.
  • Data Ownership & Portability: You maintain full control of your data and can export a local JSON backup at any time.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

PetVault requests device permissions solely to provide core application features:

A. Camera & Photo Library

Used to capture or select pet avatar images and scan vaccination certificates or medical bills. Image text extraction (OCR) occurs on-device, and photos are saved locally.

B. Local Notifications

Used to trigger local reminder notifications on your device for upcoming vaccine expirations and recurring medication schedules. No remote push server is involved.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Advertising

PetVault integrates standard third-party services to support app operations:

  • Google AdMob: Displays banner ads on free tier screens. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA) and IP addresses to serve ads and prevent ad fraud. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: Manages in-app Pro subscriptions. Transaction data and payment methods are securely handled by Google Play Store. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device as long as PetVault is installed. Deleting individual records in-app or uninstalling the PetVault application will permanently delete all local SQLite records and cached media files from your device.
